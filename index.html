◈ The Mind Behind the Machine

Standing Between
Two Worlds

Most people use technology. Sourav imagines what it could become. While others think about apps, he thinks about ecosystems. While others want tools, he wants intelligence.

On one side lives the artist — someone who notices color tones, cinematic feeling, interface beauty. He even created his own visual grading style — the "001 code" — because normal presets weren't enough to match the mood he wanted.

On the other, the builder — thinking structurally, systematically. Autonomous AI agents. Immersive portfolio operating systems. Intelligent systems that can think, act, automate, and evolve.
